## Prompt da concept art

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a gorgeous detailed pixel art concept of the INTERIOR of "Bodega do Sadi", a rural Rio Grande do Sul Brazilian tavern/shop and xis burger kitchen, in the SAME artistic language as supplied home reference: warm worn timber, earth and stone textures, grounded handcrafted furniture, careful contact shadows, high raised top-down orthographic RPG view, complete cutaway room, clearly visible tops and fronts, no isometric diamond. Wide landscape composition with rich cohesive amber atmosphere, sharp pixel clusters not blurry or photoreal.
Left half working kitchen: rear wall wooden ingredient bins with bread, lettuce tomatoes cheese and salami; large hot flat grill and sandwich press; small refrigerator, egg jars, herbs and groceries, a side wood-fired stove. A practical central assembly island with a raw unpressed xis and pressed toasted xis, sauce bottles and chopping board; generous walking aisles. Separate front service counter and merchandise counter holding erva mate paper packages, bergamotas, pinhao baskets, jars of quail eggs and pickles. Daylight through an upper window shows countryside. Sadi is the mustachioed dark-haired gaucho host in white shirt, red neckerchief, beige apron, brown boots, carrying a tray naturally at waist height; a few gaucho patrons and one pair playing cards bring life without crowding. Keep people secondary and match same scale as furnishings. Architectural details and props feel placed by someone living here. Cozy functional composition, readable navigation, refined pixel art, no UI, no counters or game HUD, no text except a tasteful wooden sign 'BODEGA DO SADI' high on the back wall. This is a preview concept, so compose a beautiful inviting complete scene.
