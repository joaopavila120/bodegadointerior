# Prompts dos sprites

Ferramenta: image_gen integrada. Cada PNG foi gerado separadamente, com transparent_background=true.

## Estilo comum

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated pixel-art inventory sprite for a Brazilian countryside tavern cooking game. Crisp chunky 32-bit era pixel art, limited warm earthy palette, dark brown 1-2 pixel outline, square stepped pixel edges, flat clustered shading with 3 tones per material, top-left light, slight three-quarter front view. Designed to remain identifiable at 24 to 48 pixels in game. Single item centered, entire subject visible, occupy 85% of square canvas, generous transparent margin. Actual transparent alpha background, no ground, no scene, no text, no letters, no watermark, no frames, no extra unrelated items. Not a photograph, not vector art, no smooth gradients or blur.
