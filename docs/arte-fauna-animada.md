## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. A production ANIMATED SPRITE SHEET for a charming southern Brazilian 2D pixel-art RPG, TRUE transparent background, exact 4 columns by 2 rows on a 1536x1024 canvas. Eight separate frames safely centered in equal 384x512 cells, no overlap, no words, no numbers. TOP ROW: four consecutive WALK CYCLE poses of the SAME caricatured coati (quati) facing RIGHT. Russet brown fur, large expressive eyes, a comically long curious snout, plump body, short paws, very recognizable LONG UPRIGHT RINGED TAIL with alternating brown and golden rings. Feet baseline at y=420 in every top-row cell. Same head, proportions and body position in all four frames; tail gently sways. Frame 1 near front and far back legs extended forward/back; frame 2 legs pass underneath, one paw visibly raised; frame 3 opposite contact, near front and far back exchanged; frame 4 opposite passing pose, other paw visibly raised. BOTTOM ROW: four consecutive WALK CYCLE poses of the SAME cute caricatured ARMADILLO (tatu), facing RIGHT. Large curious eyes, oversized long pinkish ears, round chunky segmented grey brown armored shell, short stout little legs and narrow trailing tail. Clearly an armadillo, not a mouse. Feet baseline at y=932 in all bottom-row cells. Body and shell stay the same size; alternating feet lift and reach forward distinctly through a 4-frame loop as above. Make legs and foot positions truly different, not four copies of a static animal. All frames same scale within each species, no camera changes, whole animals including tail and ears contained in cells, at least 24px transparent padding around each frame. Art: expressive caricature but lovingly shaded crisp pixel art, dark warm outlines, tactile fur and shell clusters, readable at 80px tall, elevated side view suited to a top-down game. No giant shadows, no ground, no scenes, no accessories.
