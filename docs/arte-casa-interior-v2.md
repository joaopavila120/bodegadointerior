# Interior da casa — segunda composição

Arte completa produzida com imagegen integrado (built-in), com quarto, cozinha e canto de descanso na mesma perspectiva. O cenário usa colisões e máscaras de oclusão alinhadas à ilustração, mantendo a cama funcional.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production background for the INSIDE of the player's small rural Rio Grande do Sul frontier home, for an elevated top-down 2D pixel-art RPG. One cohesive professionally art-directed room, not an asset sheet or scattered objects. Output landscape 3:2. Orthographic elevated FRONT view like Stardew Valley interiors; parallel horizontal edges, NO isometric diamond, NO photographic perspective. Crisp pixel clusters and warm restrained earth palette. No characters, no UI, no text, no roof. The whole interior and wall perimeter must fit inside image, black/dark brown void outside.
Architecture: rectangular cozy single-storey home. Back wall at top with honey cream limewashed adobe above dark wood wainscoting, thick rustic timber framing, two small green shutter windows with lace curtains admitting soft warm light. Floor is smooth compacted EARTH, muted desaturated warm umber clay, subtly swept, very LOW contrast, NOT orange noise, NOT tiles or parquet. Room floor takes x about 7%-93%, y about 24%-90%. Front/bottom wall is a LOW cutaway timber wall, with a wide walkable open doorway precisely at horizontal center of bottom edge.
Layout: left third is sleeping corner, right two thirds living/kitchen. A short vertical timber partition at about 40% width from back wall to halfway depth defines bedroom but leaves a generous opening below. Left back corner double wooden bed oriented headboard at top, foot toward viewer, pillow, woven red-green-cream wool blanket and pelego at foot. Next to bed one small bedside cabinet. A tall wardrobe placed against the back wall LEFT of partition. Modest woven floor rug at foot of bed and low closed chest along left wall near bottom-left, never blocking passage.
Kitchen: against back right wall, a blue-green wooden crockery dresser, short wooden worktop with ceramic water jug and bowls, brick wood-burning stove at FAR RIGHT with dark iron cooktop, kettle, chimney, tiny fire glow, stack of wood. A small wood table with mate gourd and thermos in right-center of floor, two small stools tucked in, top surfaces visible. Lower-right wall has a saddle stand with lasso, and wall peg for hat and poncho. A leather armchair and small woven mat at left edge of living area below partition, facing inward, enough space around it.
Carefully composed lived-in details, small sepia family photo, enamel pots, folded blanket, wall lantern. Gentle contact shadows anchor objects, warm localized stove light, thin darker wall-floor edges. Open continuous walking aisle from centered bottom doorway to center room, bedroom bed foot and kitchen table. Furniture must be proportionate to adult RPG characters around 1/7 of image height: bed about 1/4 image height, tables and chairs plausible human scale. Furnishings occupy edges with balanced useful center space, never a huge empty warehouse. Every object shares SAME pixel size, lighting and elevated camera angle. Make it feel inhabited and lovingly cared for, understated rustic campeiro home.
